# The 49 cops elysium and RuboCop are compared over in
# ci/corpus/run.sh and docs/benchmarks/phase3-vs-rubocop.md. One cop per
# line; blank lines and lines starting with `#` are ignored when run.sh
# joins this into a `--only` CSV list.
#
# This is 49 of elysium's 50 stable+nursery cops. Lint/RedundantCopDisableDirective
# is excluded: RuboCop's CLI hard-rejects `--only Lint/RedundantCopDisableDirective`
# (exit 2) because that cop depends on every other cop's reported offenses and
# can't run in isolation under `--only`.
#
# Corpus apps compared over these cops (ci/corpus/*/): discourse and
# mastodon, both pinned to RuboCop 1.91.0. forem is deliberately not a
# corpus app: it pins RuboCop 1.63.4, and docs/conformance/rules.md's
# per-app breakdown (rows annotated `[^skew-*]`) shows several of these
# cops behave differently under 1.63 than under the 1.82+ semantics
# elysium implements, so a forem mismatch would often mean "RuboCop
# changed" rather than "elysium is wrong".
